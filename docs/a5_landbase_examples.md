## Dual-Land Priority Order

1. ABU dual land
   Note: dual lands that tap for two colors and enter untapped with no condition.
2. Fetchland
   Note: pay 1 life and sacrifice to find a land of one of two types.
3. Shockland
   Note: pay 2 life, enters untapped.
4. Bondland
   Note: enters untapped in multiplayer.
5. Surveil land
   Note: surveils when entering, enters tapped.
6. Slowland
   Note: enters untapped if you have two or more other lands.
7. Verge land
   Note: taps for a second color if you control a specific land type.
8. Filter land
   Note: put in colored mana, get out a combination of colors.
9. Painland
   Note: can tap for two colors at the cost of one life per tap.
10. Canopy land
   Note: taps for two colors at the cost of life, can be sacrificed to draw a card.
11. Bounce land
   Note: taps for two mana, enters tapped and returns a land to hand.
12. Checkland
   Note: enters untapped if you have certain land types.
13. Signet land
   Note: pay one generic mana to get two colored mana. 
14. Pathway
   Note: two sides, each side taps for only one color. <br>
**The remaining lands should only be included when there are heavy budget constraints (eg. budget: low) or at the very end of a two-color deck to fill out the dual lands ONLY if needed:** 
15. Temple
   Note: enters tapped and scrys.
16. Swamp land
   Note: taps for a second color if you control a Swamp.
17. Tango land
   Note: enters untapped if you have two or more basic lands.

**Important Note:**
    Don't include reveal lands, which are lands that say "this land enters tapped unless you reveal a _ or _" where the _'s are filled in with a basic land type like forest and plains. 

## Global Landbase Rules

- Minimum 1 basic of each color.
- Cap utility-land count based on number of colors in the deck.

### Utility-Land Caps

- One color: 7 utility lands
- Two colors: 5 utility lands
- Three colors: 3 utility lands
- Four or five colors: 2 utility lands

### Fetch-Land Rule

- Fetch lands must be searched for separately.
- Only search fetch lands if budget is `high` or better.
- scryfall search is "is:fetchland"
- Only include on-color fetch lands. This means if the CID is wub, you should get:
  - the fetchland that finds a plains or island
  - the fetchland that finds a plains or swamp
  - the fetchland that finds an island or swamp
  - no other fetchlands.
